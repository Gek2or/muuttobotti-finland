# Muuttobotti v2.2 AI Trust Layer

Goal: make APK and Web feel calm, transparent and intelligent without hiding uncertainty or inventing data.

## Sprint 1 — Calm Center
- Personalized assistant card on client home.
- Status-aware next step for active orders.
- Trust indicators: booking saved, crew assignment state, price offer state, human support available.
- Clear escalation to call/WhatsApp.
- Multilingual copy FI/EN/RU/UK.

## Sprint 2 — Smart preparation
- Dynamic moving checklist based on service, date, floors, heavy items and vehicle capacity.
- Countdown to moving day.
- Warnings for missing information before the move.
- “You are ready” readiness score based only on known booking data.

## Sprint 3 — AI assistant
- Server-side assistant endpoint with strict booking context and no invented order data.
- Suggested questions and concise answers.
- Human handoff when confidence is low.
- No API keys in APK/Web clients.

## Sprint 4 — Live confidence
- ETA when available from real location/operations data.
- Crew identity/contact card.
- Timeline explanations in human language.
- Proactive delay/change notices.

All features ship from `mobile/` so Expo Web and Android stay in parity.

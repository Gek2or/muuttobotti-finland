# Muuttobotti UI polish v4

This pass deliberately preserves the existing site concept, hero artwork and brand palette.

Design goals:
- stronger Scandinavian product/service visual hierarchy
- consistent spacing, radii and typography
- premium but practical cards and forms
- calculator as the primary conversion product
- tracking preview presented as a product feature
- calmer Google review and FAQ presentation
- refined mobile behavior and accessible focus/reduced motion
- no replacement of the existing Muuttobotti brand colors or core art direction
